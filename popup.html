How it works

  1. Open any site with a video player and press play.
  2. Turn on Japanese captions, use Load sub (.srt/.vtt/.ass), or open Subs & ASR to search Jimaku, auto-sync timing, or run Live ASR.
  3. Tap any word for definitions, pitch accent diagrams, grammar breakdowns, and kanji lookalikes. Drag the caption box handle (⋮⋮) to reposition it.
  4. Use Mine line to capture screenshots, real show audio, pitch accent, and send to Anki, or practice speaking with Shadow (Mic).
